The Commodore 64 classic Raid on Bungeling Bay, in 3D. 

The object of the game is to bomb all six factories with your attack helicopter.

## Version 3.0.0 new features:

  * Low poly water and terrain.  The vertex count is so low now its possible to migrate this game to mobile platforms.
  * You can equip 6 Air-to-Air missiles to take down enemy bombers and fighters.
  * Carrier now highlights landing area on deck.
  * AA guns shoot very fast, but do lower damage.  They also have increased range.
  * All bullets use lead bullet firing, so both you and the enemy can dodge bullets.
  * Cruise missile warning tone added.  Shooting down cruise missiles is much harder, but it's score increased to 250.
  * In-game menu is now mobile like.
  * You can now crash into mountains. Try it!
